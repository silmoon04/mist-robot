## Minimum changes before new walking-policy training

The bounded MSI device screen uses the frozen experiment for runtime timing; its fresh smoke checkpoints are diagnostic artifacts. The changes below concern the next controller's qualification training.

Keep `simulation-walking-v2` acceptance and failed cases visible. The existing nominal four-direction unit test requires 2 cm progress in eight seconds; it does not establish velocity/path/yaw tracking. New development comparisons need full-duration tracking evidence and separate validation/final seed manifests. Do not loosen thresholds to make a gait pass.

Clarify torque semantics in the new actuator contract. The proxy computes available torque as the nominal cap multiplied by strength, voltage and thermal factors. At upper training bounds, a 0.588 N m nominal cap can reach about 0.710 N m before friction. State the nominal and absolute envelopes separately; identify the owned servo's raw torque/current settings against measurements. A firmware percentage is not a measured sustainable joint torque.

## Measurements before hardware walking

Use `profiles/r7_measurements.template.json`, the R7 calibration example and sensor-mount example. Record conditions, instruments, raw evidence and hashes. The shipped profiles remain unconfigured.

- Measure all twelve servo IDs, reference angle/tick, direction and safe ends with margin within one non-wrapping revolution. Confirm held-pose arming, the normally closed stop loop and board watchdog timing.
- Weigh printed links, hub, battery, electronics/harness and complete phone mount. Measure assembled COM with phone present/absent. Check R7 DFM changes, fasteners, cable travel, inter-leg clearance and the R5 phone cradle's fit on the revised hub.
- Measure loaded step/sine response, delay, slew, backlash, voltage dependence, raw speed/current/load interpretation and sustained torque/temperature. Start with supported single-leg and standing/load tests. The thermal proxy and 540 g harness placement are estimates.
- Measure fixed MPU and phone mounting rotations, stationary bias, vibration response, timestamp pairing and transport/inference/command delay. Arrival freshness and an inferred clock offset do not establish absolute end-to-end latency.

Fresh synthetic training can proceed after the software contract is settled and its assumptions are recorded. Physical transfer remains gated on these measurements and supported tests. There are no foot sensors or vision; servo load/current can become contact hints only after their behavior is measured. The strongest unresolved issue is whether planned touchdown/support timing, real foot compliance and loaded actuator response jointly maintain tracking on offset-COM and sloped configurations.

## Reuse beyond R7

The profile audit supports alternate serial geometry analysis, but the exporter deliberately accepts only pinned R7 sources. `model.py` retains fixed neutral/mount/toe assumptions, and `gait.py` embeds 82/90 mm gravity lever arms. For another robot, introduce one validated specification supplying geometry, mounts, limits, inertials, actuator and timing contracts to model, controller and hardware adapter. Preserve fail-closed source checks. Require model/FK, collision/clearance and calibration evidence before treating an alternate profile as an executable robot.
