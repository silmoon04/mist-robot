## Verdict — be decisive

**Best pick on ~£150: buy the Creality Ender 3 V3 SE new at £149.** It is the *only* machine that ticks every hard requirement at budget — direct-drive (mandatory for TPU 95A feet), full CR-Touch auto-leveling (huge for a first printer), 220×220 bed, 260C hotend (fine for PETG), plus the biggest community and cheapest Prime spares. Warranty included. Don't chase a used SV06 or a phantom £169 Neptune sale to save nothing.

**One caveat the adversary is right about — measure before you buy.** No hexapod CAD exists in the workspace, so "220 fits the body" is unproven. A 220mm bed is plenty for **legs, brackets, feet, and plates**, but after brim/skew/placement margin you realistically have ~200–205mm of usable flat span. **Design the chassis modular/split, OR confirm the body's longest flat dimension is ≤~200mm before committing.** If it must be one-piece and larger, that's a Bambu A1 decision.

**Best used bargain to hunt near E11:** a **converted** Ender 3 / 3 Pro / V2 that *already* has a direct-drive kit + BLTouch/CR-Touch fitted, **only if under ~£70 and it prints a TPU sample in front of you.** Otherwise skip used entirely — a stock classic Ender (Bowden + manual leveling) is the exact wrong learning stack for TPU, and a used V3 SE at £90–120 is too close to £149 new to bother. When in doubt, buy the new SE.

**Stretch option (worth it only if the body must be one-piece): full Bambu Lab A1 at ~£234 (sale) / £299 (list)** — the 256mm bed swallows the hexapod body without splitting, prints PETG + TPU well, and is the most reliable out-of-box. Budget it honestly as a ~£234–299 machine, **not** £150. **Do not confuse it with the A1 Mini** (180mm bed — too small).

## The two gotchas that decide this build

1. **TPU 95A demands DIRECT DRIVE.** Bowden machines (stock classic Ender 3, Anycubic Kobra Go) buckle flexible TPU in the tube. Every pick above is direct-drive; that's non-negotiable for the feet. Also: dry the filament, print slow (20–30 mm/s), low/zero retraction.
2. **BUILD VOLUME is unverified — measure the CAD.** ~220mm is comfortable for parts, borderline for a one-piece body. Confirm the chassis fits ~200mm usable, or split it, or step up to the A1's 256mm bed.

**Bottom line:** New **Ender 3 V3 SE at £149** is the pick — verify your chassis bounding box first. Stretch to the **full Bambu A1 (~£234)** only if the body must print in one piece.
