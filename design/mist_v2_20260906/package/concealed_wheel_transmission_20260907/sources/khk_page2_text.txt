Miter gears are a special class of bevel gears where the shafts intersect at 90° and the gear ratio is 1:1.

KHK stock miter gears are available in two types, straight miter and spiral miter, with high precision grade for demanding 

torques and speeds, and commercial grade for economical applications. The following table lists the main features for easy
