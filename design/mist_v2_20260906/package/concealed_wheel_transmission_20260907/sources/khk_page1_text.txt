Product Precautions Page 274

[Caution on Product Characteristics] ① Because this is extruded material, the outer diameter may be out of H9 tolerance in sections.

[Caution on Secondary Operations] ① Avoid hardening round racks, due to twisting and deformation occurring and the difficulty of straightening the rack after 

hardening.

[Caution on Product Characteristics] ① When using the metal flexible rack in an arc, the minimum bending radius (R) is 150 mm for both the external and 

internal teeth.

 Using a smaller radius increases the pitch errors and tooth profile errors which prevent the teeth from meshing at the 

normal center distance, so be sure to make adjustments before use.

② The tolerance of height (size C) is 0 to -0.15, and the tolerance of base width (size F) is 0 to -0.1.

③ It cannot be used where positioning accuracy is required.
