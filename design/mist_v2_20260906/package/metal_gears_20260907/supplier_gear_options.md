# Metal module 1 gear options

Checked 7 September 2026. Scope: 20:40 and 25:50 spur pairs, with an 8 mm face-width preference; 7 mm output shaft interface; an 8 mm knee gear slice at y = 28.125–36.125 mm coupled to the selected MISUMI 3GT pulley. No purchases or supplier contacts were made. Only this note was written for this subtask.

HPC's plain PG1 gears are the closest catalogue match to the 8 mm slice. The 25T input leaves more material around the servo horn bolt circle than 20T. A 7/8 mm finished bore and both axial bolt patterns still require a specified modification or adapter. No catalogue gear below is claimed to contain the C018 spline, four M3 holes on 14 mm BCD, or four M4 holes on 16 mm PCD.

## Interface screening for the supplied robot geometry

The supplied C018 information is a 25T spline approximately 5.9 mm OD and a factory horn approximately 19.95 mm OD with four M3 holes on 14 mm BCD. Tooth count and spline OD alone do not define a machinable mating spline. The practical candidate retains the factory horn and uses a registered, bolted metal gear/adapter.

For centre distance, standard unshifted module 1 geometry gives `(20 + 40)/2 = 30 mm` and `(25 + 50)/2 = 37.5 mm`. These are nominal centres; backlash and the chosen manufacturer's tooth tolerances still matter.

For root-clearance screening only, assuming unshifted 1.25-module dedendum gives root diameters 17.5, 22.5, 37.5 and 47.5 mm for 20, 25, 40 and 50 teeth. These are calculated reference circles, not verified manufacturer root contours. Fillets, modifications and tolerances must come from the final drawing/CAD.

Four 3.4 mm M3 clearance holes on 14 mm BCD have a radial outer envelope of 17.4 mm diameter. Against that nominal root circle, 20T leaves just 0.05 mm radial material; 25T leaves 2.55 mm. Consequently a direct 20T face pattern is not a credible released design from these dimensions. M3 threaded holes or smaller drills still need root/fillet checks and useful edge distance. Counterbores need a separate, larger envelope. The 19.95 mm horn also projects beyond a 20T nominal root diameter, so horn position and mating-gear clearance must be checked in 3D. A 15 mm catalogue boss cannot fully contain this bolt pattern.

For the knee, a plain HPC PG1-50's 8 mm thickness can occupy y = 28.125–36.125 mm before added spacers or heads. The selected pulley modification is four M4 holes on 16 mm PCD; the gear needs its own separately specified matching holes and a concentric register. On a stock 10 mm gear bore, 4.5 mm clearance holes on that circle leave only `8 − 2.25 − 5 = 0.75 mm` between bore and hole edges. A factory 8 mm bore increases this to 1.75 mm. These narrow ligaments and the sleeve/register arrangement need checking; the catalogue supplies no rating for this face-bolted assembly.

For a 7 mm shaft, HPC's stock 8 mm G1/PG1-40 likewise needs a custom 7 mm bore or designed adapter. Use the face bolts and registered carrier as an explicit torque path to the chosen shaft clamp. A slip-fit bore or axial collar alone does not establish low-play torque transfer.

## Concrete quotation specification

Preferred dimensional starting point: **HPC PG1-25 + PG1-50**, steel, module 1, 20° PA, 8 mm finished face; retain 37.5 mm nominal centres pending mesh checks. Specify the actual input horn register and four M3/14 mm BCD holes from measured CAD, and the output's required 7 or 8 mm bore plus four M4/16 mm PCD interface, with hole type/depth, clocking, register and fastener-head side explicit. Do not label these as stock features.

Ask for the final drawing/STEP including actual root/fillet geometry, material condition and tooth quality, machined bore/face tolerances, minimum allowable web ligaments, price and dispatch for the modified gears. Gear tooth capacity, shaft-hub holding capacity and the face-bolt connection require separate checks. No supplier rating has been assigned to the proposed modified assembly in this note.
