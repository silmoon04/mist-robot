FOURTH LEG

Print each of these two plates once. Together they provide all ten remaining
printed components for one complete leg. Reuse four of your existing matching
13 mm tall, 5.5 mm outside diameter spacers. No extra spacers or hub are needed.

Plate 1: 01_Main_Parts_With_Supports.stl
Estimated 6 h 14 min, about 109 g PLA.
Contains coxa upper/hip saddle, femur main/knee saddle, and tibia main/foot.
Includes the corrected removable supports beneath both femur saddle levels,
the coxa post regions, and the raised tibia shank.

Plate 2: 02_Brackets_And_Caps.stl
Estimated 4 h 41 min, about 78 g PLA.
Contains yaw servo saddle, yaw cap, lower coxa arm, hip cap, femur auxiliary
cheek, knee cap and tibia auxiliary cheek. See Plate_2_Map.png.

Total estimate: about 10 h 55 min and 187 g PLA, including support and adhesion
material. Heating and differences in actual printer motion are not included.
The full set is split to leave room for the supports and brim within the bed.

USE THE SAME PROFILE FOR BOTH PLATES

Import and select Neptune4Pro_Recovery_Balanced_028.curaprofile in Cura.
Keep your existing Elegoo Neptune 4 Pro machine and startup configuration,
0.4 mm nozzle and PLA. Import one plate at a time, at 100% scale, in its supplied
orientation. Each 3MF is an alternative to its matching STL, not an extra plate.
The STL keeps the arranged geometry together; do not remove its support fins.
Loading a model does not automatically activate the profile.

The profile uses 0.28 mm layers, five walls, 25% gyroid infill, a 6 mm outside
brim with 0.1 mm gap, and the slower recovery first layer and acceleration.
Inner walls/infill are 90 mm/s, outer walls 50 mm/s and top/bottom 60 mm/s.
Nozzle temperature is 210 C initially then 215 C, with the bed at 60 C.
Automatic tree support remains enabled. Remove the orange supports shown in
the pictures after printing, retaining the main ribs and integral print feet.

ASSEMBLY

The part interfaces match the latest insert-compatible third-leg revision.
Use three M3 heat inserts, 4 mm outside diameter and 5 mm long: one at the
femur bridge and two at the tibia bridge. Fit them square and flush and let
them cool before fitting the auxiliary cheeks. Each bridge connection uses
an M3x10 screw with one 0.5 mm flat washer through the 5 mm cheek. Do not use
the previous M3x30 bridge screws at these inserts. The nine servo case-clamp
screws remain M3x30 with nuts. Metal horn fasteners remain unchanged.

Use the matching spacer/hardware arrangement that you confirmed turns freely
on your assembled legs. No new yaw axial-stack calculation is claimed here.
You also need three STS3215 servos, the matching horns/auxiliary discs and
their retainers, cables and the remaining assembly fasteners.

CHECKS

Plate 1 and the profile are exact copies of the checked balanced recovery
files. Plate 2 was newly packed and sliced; its closed meshes and sliced bed
envelope passed checks. No automatic support centreline midpoints were found
inside the defined openings checked on that plate. These are bounded geometry
and toolpath checks, not proof of adhesion or support removal force.

The physical result of the balanced recovery print has not yet been reported.
Clean and reseat the PEI sheet as planned and inspect the first layers. The
higher flow and added supports remain subject to physical confirmation.
This package is a prototype print set, not a new full-robot load qualification.
