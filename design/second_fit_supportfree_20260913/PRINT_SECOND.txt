IMPORT AND SLICE
1. Select the Neptune 4 Pro with a 0.4 mm nozzle and PLA in Cura 5.13.
2. Preferences > Configure Cura > Profiles > Import. Choose the supplied
   Neptune4Pro_SecondFit_NoSupports_028.curaprofile.
3. Activate R5 Second Fit - No Supports - 0.28mm. If Cura asks to retain old
   print-setting overrides, choose Discard Changes so old supports stay off.
4. Clear the previous plate. Import Second_Print_Full_Clamp_NoSupports.3mf as
   models/geometry, preserving this profile. Keep its scale and orientations.
5. Confirm Generate Support is OFF. Slice, then inspect the pointed hole roofs
   in layer preview. There should be no support structures inside the holes.
   The brim around the bottoms is intentional and is not hole support.
6. Keep the printer speed and flow multipliers at 100% for this comparison.

GEOMETRY CHANGES
The three screw passages preserve the original 3.4 mm circular clearance and
their original axes. A small pointed extension above each circle creates
45-degree roofs. These are teardrop-style screw holes, not smaller hex holes.
The nut pockets preserve the 5.7 mm locating flats and lower hex faces, with
additional 45-degree roof clearance above them. A 5.5 mm AF plain M3 nut fits
nominally; a 10-degree rotation is blocked by the remaining pocket faces.
The two pockets near the top receive small external roof tabs to retain at
least 2 mm of vertical roof material. The original very thin roofs are not
left weaker by simply enlarging their holes.
The servo-contact faces, case width/length, end clearance, clamp gap, mounting
feet and every screw axis are unchanged. CAD differences within the fitted
case region are zero. No heat-set inserts are used.

WHAT TO TEST AFTER THIS PRINT
1. Remove only the brim and loose strings. Inspect each roof for sagging or
   a hanging strand before trying hardware. Do not force a screw through it.
2. Try an M3 screw through each empty saddle and cap hole by hand, without the
   servo fitted. Record which hole is clear or obstructed before drilling or
   sanding; we need to distinguish print sag from nominal hole size.
3. Fit each plain M3 nut in its pocket. It should seat without heat or force
   and resist turning when the screw engages. Check all three positions.
4. Insert the actual servo and add the cap. Use the same nominal clamp stack:
   three M3x35 screws, one OD7/ID3.2 x 0.5 mm head washer per screw, and three
   plain M3 nuts, nominally 5.5 mm across flats and 2.4 mm thick.
   Tighten gently and evenly, only enough to check retention. Inspect for cap
   bottoming, nut rotation, case movement, roof cracking and blocked cables.
5. Report roof cleanliness, screw insertion, nut seating and cap grip. A photo
   before cleanup is useful if a roof or hole still fails.

Both printable parts are single valid solids with watertight meshes. The 3MF
reloads as two objects. No non-bed downward mesh face exceeds the intended
45-degree overhang. Nominal screws, nuts and servo fit in the CAD, and the
