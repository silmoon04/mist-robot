R4 FIRST-FIT CHECKS

Disposition: controlled first-fit printing. The corrected digital geometry, phone access and staged clamp-nut checks are complete. Full production remains gated on successful physical fixtures and one-leg tests. The earlier R3 interface hold is archived under manufacturing/baseline_r3/printing.

1. Start with the 16-piece first-print plate: 13 fit pieces and three section specimens required before loading. Alternative fits remain available separately. Read PRINT_FIRST.txt and PRINT_AND_ASSEMBLE.md before slicing.

2. Verify the actual clamped case and shaft together in the registration gauge. The shaft offset is 12.5 mm from the CASE MIDPOINT. The compatible supplier shell and digital alignment checks do not measure the delivered servo, spline seating or cable plug.

3. Preload all nine clamp nuts per leg in their bare carriers. Two front hip nuts need staged side entry. The verified nominal nut-flat clearance is only 0.1 mm. Seven nut-service interference pairs remain in the fully assembled model: remove the surrounding femur/lower-arm access obstructions before replacement. Do not force a nut or assume assembled service access.

4. Check actual washer thickness, screw tips and free auxiliary rotation. Pitch auxiliary screws are M3x8 with TWO OD6 x 0.5 mm washers and integral 1.1 mm lands. Yaw uses M3x20, paired OD6 washers, 3.5 mm counterbores and nominal 11.6 mm spacers. Each auxiliary centre retainer uses its specified OD7 washer against the fixed stub. Both peripheral auxiliary stacks have only 0.2 mm nominal rear margin.

5. Tighten the pan body and servo-cap screws before installing the horn/hub. Fit a dummy landscape phone before the real Mi9SE. Check side USB access with pan parked and disabled.

6. Use captured, incremental load tests with measured force, lever arm, displacement, twist and residual set. The cropped post coupon excludes the critical access cut near Z=-25. The structural test therefore needs the complete coxa frame, actual upper yaw plate and lower arm, with vertical load at the hip and independently reversible Y torque. A vertical-only calculation cannot qualify full hip torque or disturbance loads.

Stop for forced alignment, binding, bottomed screws, cap or nut-roof lift, cracks, whitening, slip, progressive deflection or permanent set. Repeat relevant fit and fixture tests after any geometry, material or print-process change. Full-robot loaded motion and warm/creep performance remain unqualified.
