# Cable clearance reported by the operator

The operator measured approximately 34.8 to 35 mm across the servo, including the rear bulge, without the cable. The plugged cable adds about 1.5 mm. The wire is flexible and stays close to the rear profile; the operator considers 2 mm additional clearance adequate, with 2 to 3 mm a sensible allowance.

These are approximate overall measurements. They are not an axial measurement from the main bearing face, so do not add 35 mm to that datum or turn the value into a precise connector offset. Use the supplier case surface and the corrected C018 datum when positioning the clearance, then check the actual plugged assembly.

This replaces the earlier arbitrary 15 mm longitudinal-tail cable reservation. The connectors are on the auxiliary shaft face and extract along the shaft direction. The manufacturer drawing also places the shaft 12.5 mm from the case midpoint, giving nominal long/short distances of 35.115 and 10.115 mm.
