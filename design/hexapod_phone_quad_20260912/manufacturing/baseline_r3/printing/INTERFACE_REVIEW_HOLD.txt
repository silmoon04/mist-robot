Servo interface correction in progress

Do not use the existing R3 exports as a qualified servo fit release. The inward-knee investigation identified an incorrect case-axis datum and an incorrect assumed connector direction in that revision. The correct shaft offset is 12.5 mm from the case midpoint; the cable exits the auxiliary face.

An isolated corrected inward-knee candidate and corrected phone-pan clamp are being checked before production files are regenerated. The R3 files and simulation remain useful historical model-consistency evidence, but do not validate the corrected physical interface.

The operator's actual cable measurement supports a 2 to 3 mm allowance beyond the plugged rear profile. The new fit coupon will check case retention and shaft alignment together.
