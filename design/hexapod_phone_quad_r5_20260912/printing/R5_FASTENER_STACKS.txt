# R5 fastener stacks and assembly checks

Use this table with the R5 inward hip and knee geometry, straight tibia and corrected C018 shaft datum. Dimensions are nominal millimetres. A successful solid-intersection check does not establish screw engagement, nut capture, washer seating, driver access or free auxiliary rotation. Check those interfaces with the delivered hardware before copying the first assembled leg.

R5 places the hip axis 70 mm from the yaw axis. The lower-arm post bolts are at X28.5, Y-4.5 and Y4.5. The dedicated hip cap moves its rear bolt to servo-local X-38.915, Z-27.5; the other cap bolts remain at X13.915, Z-23 and Z-7. Do not substitute a yaw or knee cap at the hip. The straight tibia bridge bolts are at X36 and X44, both Z0. Screw lengths, nut sizes, auxiliary washers and total hardware quantities remain unchanged from R4.

The purchased screw plan contains 200 screws: 36 M3x35, 16 M3x12, 52 M3x6, 32 M3x8, 24 M3x20, 12 M3x50, 20 M3x10 and 8 M3x16. It also requires 100 plain M3 nuts and 256 washers. Washers are ID3.2, 0.5 thick: 148 have OD6 and 108 have OD7. The 13 supplied main M3x6 and 12 supplied auxiliary PA3.0x5 retainers are counted separately. Quantities exclude spare and coupon-only hardware.

The pitch auxiliary screws enter along +Y from an under-head plane at Y-24 and end at Y-16. The flange spans Y-17.9 to Y-15.8, leaving only 0.2 mm nominal rear margin. One washer would make these M3x8 screws project 0.3 mm behind the flange. Keep both washers and measure the assembled stack.

The yaw auxiliary counterbore floor is Z-49.5. Two washers place the under-head plane at Z-50.5 and the M3x20 tip at Z-30.5. Its flange spans Z-32.4 to Z-30.3, again leaving 0.2 mm nominal rear margin. An OD7 washer does not fit the 6.2 mm peripheral counterbores. Do not substitute washer sizes or screw lengths without repeating this check.

Each auxiliary center washer bears on the fixed stub tip at servo-local Z-33.1. Its center is Z-33.35, and the PA screw starts at Z-33.6. The disc face is Z-32.4, giving 0.7 mm nominal axial gap to the washer. The 7 mm washer overlaps the 6.05 mm bore by 0.475 mm radially. After the washer, the 5 mm screw has 4.5 mm of nominal entry; this does not prove the actual pilot-hole depth or threaded engagement. Verify the head covers the washer bore, the washer clears the printed opening and the disc rotates freely when the screw is seated against the stub.

Both hip and knee cases now point inward. Their clamp screws enter from below in their respective parent-link frames. Check screwdriver access with the complete femur and tibia in place. Inspect the exposed M3x50 heads, nuts and tips through the permitted motion; the tibia's 8 mm bridge-hole spacing leaves only 1 mm between OD7 washers. Load the coxa's staggered nuts through their side windows before attaching the lower arm. Stop for binding, bottomed screws, washers sitting on support residue, nut pockets that cannot react tension, or parts that need forced spreading.

The distributed mass model estimates 273.946 to 390.991 g for this hardware, with 334.673 g nominal. It assigns the auxiliary center screws and washers to the fixed servo housing links. Replace the old fastener allowance rather than adding both. Weigh actual hardware, and resolve whether the stated bare servo mass already includes the supplied center screws, before final loaded qualification.
