WHY THESE EXTRAS
The lower yaw arm uses the standard C018 auxiliary-disc screw pattern and the
nominal yaw spacers already on the plate. It lets you test the lower support
stack without committing to an entire moving leg. Its fit still needs checking.
The four phone-pan foot spacers have simple geometry and standard clearance
bores. Keep them labelled for the later pan module. They do not go in the
horn fixture or replace the 11.6 mm yaw spacers or 5 mm gauge spacers.

ASSEMBLY ORDER
Follow the existing four assembly images for the alignment gauge and horn
fixture. Use image 05 and ASSEMBLY_ADDITIONS.txt for the extra lower arm.
Fit the auxiliary disc and its central retainer before adding
the lower arm, because the arm can obstruct that work. Keep power disconnected;
this is an unloaded fit test, not a complete supported leg or gait test.

The five additions were selected as lower-risk standard-interface parts,
not as guaranteed final fits. No structural qualification or CAD clearance
result is being inferred from the successful case-clamp fit alone.
