LOWER YAW SUPPORT ARM
Reuse the clamped servo. If the two-sided horn fixture is still attached,
remove that fixture before this lower-support test. Keep power disconnected.
Fit the supplied auxiliary disc on the fixed rear stub. Fit its PA3x5 centre
retainer with one OD7/ID3.2 x 0.5 mm washer bearing on the stub, not on the
rotating disc. Check the disc remains free before installing the lower arm.
Do this first because the arm's centre opening can obstruct later access.

Place FOUR matching nominal 11.6 mm yaw spacers between the disc and the arm.
Use the four-hole pattern around the axis, not the outer pair of post holes.
The arm's recessed screw seats face AWAY from the servo in assembly. This is
opposite to the upward-facing recesses used while printing this new plate.

At each of the four holes use an M3x20 screw and TWO OD6/ID3.2 x 0.5 mm washers.
The two washers fit inside the 6.2 mm counterbore. An OD7 washer does not fit.
Pass each screw through the arm and its spacer into the auxiliary disc.
Nominal flange engagement is 1.9 mm, with only 0.2 mm rear tip margin.
Do not omit a washer or tighten a screw that bottoms before the stack seats.

Tighten gently in stages. With the case held still, check that the passive
auxiliary support does not bind. Stop for rubbing, cracking, washer tilt or
a spacer that fails to seat. This partial assembly has no upper coxa frame;
do not apply leg loads or command powered motion.
Leave the two outer post holes unused until the upper coxa is assembled later.

FOUR PHONE-PAN FOOT SPACERS
These added spacers are 4 mm tall. Keep them together for the four phone-pan
mounting feet in a later assembly. They do not belong in this lower-yaw test.
Do not confuse them with the two 5 mm gauge spacers or the six narrow yaw
spacers. The single 11.3 mm and 11.9 mm yaw pieces are comparison samples,
not a mixed-height replacement for the four nominal 11.6 mm pieces.

The lower-support CAD check found no positive intersections at the illustrated
position between the existing clamp, nominal servo, disc, four spacers and
lower arm. That is not a continuous motion, printed-strength or physical-fit
qualification. Record the real seating and free-movement results before
committing to the remaining full leg parts.
