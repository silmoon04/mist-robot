PROFILE AND IMPORT
Select Neptune 4 Pro, a 0.4 mm nozzle and PLA in Cura. Import the new profile
through Preferences > Configure Cura > Profiles > Import. Activate
R5 Third Fit - Joint Interfaces - 0.20mm.
Discard previous print-setting overrides if prompted, clear the old plate and
import the new 3MF as geometry. Keep its millimetre scale and orientations.
Generate Support must be OFF. Slice and inspect the gauge feet and bridge roof.
The outside brim is intentional; there should be no support inside the holes.

This batch uses 0.20 mm layers for the horn faces, spacer stack and alignment
checks. It keeps five walls and 15% gyroid. PLA starting temperatures are
210 C nozzle and 60 C bed, within the filament maker's range. First layer
25 mm/s, outer walls 50, inner walls 80 and infill 90. Overhang and bridge
slowdowns remain enabled. First-layer expansion/calibration is not changed.
The outer brim gap is 0.15 mm to help removal; no brim is added inside holes.
Keep speed and flow multipliers at 100% for the first trial.

TEST 1: HOLES AND NUT SEATING
Before sanding, note any brim lip or sag and take a photo if a fit is poor.
On the screw strip, from its notched end, the bores are 3.2, 3.4 and 3.6 mm.
On the nut strip, from its notched end, pocket depths are 2.6, 2.8 and 3.0 mm;
all have the same 5.7 mm across-flats width. Check whether the nut seats, lies
flush or stands proud, and whether it turns when an M3 screw engages.
Do not enlarge all production holes from this one result. Record which
feature needed cleanup so we can distinguish size from first-layer residue.

TEST 2: CASE-TO-SHAFT ALIGNMENT
Keep servo power disconnected. The gauge locks the output to the case;
NEVER command the servo while this alignment gauge is attached.
Keep the servo in your successful clamp. Seat the supplied main horn on its
shaft and fit its proper centre retainer without bottoming or forcing it.
Place the gauge on the horn and the two existing X9/Y+20 and X9/Y-20 clamp
feet. Its horn face and both feet should meet without pulling a gap closed.
These are the two mounting feet nearer the shaft end of the clamped case.
Each M3x50 tie bolt enters from the gauge's top plate with an OD6 washer,
passes down the straight bore and through the saddle foot, then receives
a printed 5 mm spacer, another OD6 washer and its nut underneath. With M3x45,
omit only that 5 mm spacer. Nominal nut-tip emergence is about 1.1 mm.
Use the four M3x6 screws with one OD6 washer each on the main horn. Nominal
engagement is 1.5 mm into its 2.5 mm flange. Do not pull a mismatch flat with
the tie bolts or horn screws. Record where a gap, tilt or misalignment occurs.
Remove the gauge completely after recording the fit.

TEST 3: MAIN AND AUXILIARY HORN STACK
This fixture uses the bare servo and the two supplied discs. The servo's
long case body must point AWAY from the small printed bridge, while the
two cheek arms point toward the bridge. This direction clears the case.
First check the auxiliary disc turns freely on its fixed stub before joining
it to the main side. Its centre washer seats against the fixed stub, not
against the rotating disc. Nominal disc-to-washer axial gap is 0.7 mm.
Fit the main cheek using four M3x6 screws and one OD6 washer each. Fit the
auxiliary cheek using four M3x8 screws with TWO OD6 washers each and the
integral 1.1 mm lands. The auxiliary centre passage is 7.8 mm.
Join the cheeks with the roofed 38 mm bridge, one M3x50, two OD7 washers
and a plain nut. Tighten gently in stages and watch for spreading or binding.
Once both sides are joined, the output has the servo gearbox's resistance;
do not force it to spin like a loose bearing. This is not a powered gait test.
The auxiliary cheek has a cable relief based on the existing provisional
plug envelope. Check the actual plug at this neutral fixture position.

SPACERS
The two wider, 5 mm tall pieces belong only under the gauge tie bolts.
The six narrow yaw spacers are a different set. Start with the FOUR 11.6 mm
nominal pieces. The single short and long pieces are comparison samples,
not a mixed-height set. Measure their real printed heights and keep them
labelled. Full yaw assembly will require four matching accepted spacers.
