ASSEMBLY IMAGES FOR THE CURRENT THIRD FIT PLATE

Follow 01, 02, 03, then 04. These images cover assembly only.

01_place_alignment_gauge.png
Seat the supplied main horn and its centre screw. Lower the new gauge onto
the horn and the two shaft-end feet of your already fitted clamp.

02_gauge_fastener_order.png
Each of the two M3x50 tie bolts uses an upper OD6 washer, the gauge and clamp
foot, a printed 5 mm underside spacer, another OD6 washer and an M3 nut.
If using M3x45 tie bolts instead, omit only the two printed 5 mm spacers.
Four M3x6 screws, each with one OD6 x 0.5 mm washer, connect gauge to main horn.

03_assemble_horn_fixture.png
Remove the alignment gauge first. Fit the two supplied discs and their centre
retainers, then the two printed cheeks. The long case points away from the
small bridge, while both printed cheek arms point toward it. Close the bridge
with one M3x50, two OD7 x 0.5 mm washers and one plain M3 nut.

04_washer_and_retainer_stacks.png
Main side: four M3x6 screws, one OD6 x 0.5 mm washer each.
Auxiliary side: four M3x8 screws, TWO OD6 x 0.5 mm washers each, using the
printed cheek's integral 1.1 mm lands. Do not omit those washers.
The separate auxiliary-centre PA3x5 retainer uses one OD7 x 0.5 mm washer
against the fixed stub. It must not clamp the rotating auxiliary disc.

Keep servo power disconnected. The alignment gauge locks output to case.
Check loose auxiliary-disc freedom before joining the cheeks. After joining,
the output has gearbox resistance; do not force it to spin like a loose bearing.

The printed components are rendered directly from the current CAD geometry.
Exploded spacing and fastener envelopes are illustrative. The fourth image
is a schematic section, not a dimensional drawing. Original STL, STEP and
assembly instruction files were not changed to make these illustrations.
For detailed test order and hardware quantities, use the existing PRINT_NEXT.txt
in the third_fit_joints_20260913 directory.
