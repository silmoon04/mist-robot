QUICK FIRST-FIT PRINTS FOR THE R5 QUADRUPED

Start with 01_servo_case_fit.3mf and Neptune4Pro_R5_QuickFit_PLA_020.curaprofile.
The first job is estimated at 2 hours 23 minutes. It contains the unchanged
nominal servo saddle and cap, so you can check the actual case fit first.

This replaces the order and grouping of the original first coupon plate.
It does not replace the R5 robot model, full production plate 02, or structural
qualification. All 15 original first-plate specimens remain available here.

These are CuraEngine estimates, not promised printer times. Your Cura result
can differ. All four jobs together still take about 9 h 14 min, before extra
heating and handling. The first useful result arrives much sooner, and you
can stop before printing more if the clamp fit needs changing.

HOW TO IMPORT
1. Select ELEGOO NEPTUNE 4 Pro, a 0.4 mm nozzle and PLA in Cura.
2. Preferences > Configure Cura > Profiles > Import.
3. Import Neptune4Pro_R5_QuickFit_PLA_020.curaprofile and activate
   HexQuad R5 - Quick Fit PLA - 0.20mm.
4. Choose Discard Changes if Cura asks about carrying existing print-setting
   overrides into the new profile. Save an old custom setup first if needed.
5. Clear the old nine-hour coupon plate, then load ONLY 01_servo_case_fit.3mf.
   Import geometry rather than applying unrelated project settings.
6. Keep the supplied scale and orientation. Slice with supports enabled and
   inspect the first layers, support and brim before printing.
7. Leave the printer's speed and flow multipliers at 100% during the test.

WHAT EACH JOB CHECKS
01: The nominal C018 case saddle and cap. Test with the actual servo, M3 nuts,
    washers and clamp screws. Look for hand assembly, positive case contact,
    cap travel and accessible leads. Do not force or overtighten a poor fit.
02: Reuse the saddle/cap from job 01 with the case-to-shaft registration gauge.
    This checks the critical case/shaft datum together. It also includes the
    M3 hole strip, main/auxiliary horn fixtures, their bridge, nominal yaw
    spacer and small auxiliary bridge specimen. Check actual supplied horns,
    washers and retainers for seating and free auxiliary rotation.
    A passing job 01 alone does NOT establish shaft alignment or joint fit.
03: Nominal landscape phone tray, both short clips and USB-spine specimen.
    Check the bare Mi 9 SE, retention, buttons and parked USB access.
04: Pan horn hub and main femur bridge specimen. Use with the corresponding
    hardware and auxiliary specimen from job 02 before controlled load tests.

WHAT WAS PRESERVED
No coupon mesh was resized, hollowed, cropped or simplified. Only bed placement
and rotation about the bed's vertical axis changed. The original print-face
orientation, 0.20 mm layers, 0.42 mm lines, five walls, six top/bottom layers,
35% gyroid and support settings remain. These are the same fifteen specimens,
with no duplicates or omissions.

WHAT CHANGED IN THE PROFILE
Outer walls 50 mm/s; inner walls 80; infill 90; top/bottom 45.
First layer 25 mm/s; support 60; support interface 35.
Outer-wall acceleration 1200 mm/s2; inner walls 2000; infill 2500.
PLA starting temperatures remain 210 C nozzle and 60 C bed, within the
filament's stated range. Fan, retraction and approximate 4 mm brim remain.
Normal zigzag supports are enabled, including their top interface.
Keep support beneath raised surfaces and the later alignment-gauge feet.
Inspect small nut pockets so support does not become trapped inside them.

Peak calculated deposition flow across the four jobs is 7.76 mm3/s, below
the chosen 8 mm3/s analysis target. Cura's maximum-flow setting is hidden in
this build, so the explicit speeds and line widths matter. Do not increase
layer height or line width and assume flow will remain the same.

QUALITY AND SCOPE
The earlier full-plate experiment still took about 6 h 51 min at 0.30 mm.
That experiment used a preliminary tuning profile, not this final profile.
The chosen solution keeps 0.20 mm layers and the real fitting interfaces.
Preserving geometry and layer height does not guarantee identical physical
quality: the moderately faster walls and acceleration need a first print.
If edges ring or small holes look poor, return outer walls to 35 mm/s and
outer-wall acceleration to 800 mm/s2; the estimate will increase.

Profile format, setting types and installed Cura quality metadata were checked.
Each 3MF reloads with the expected object count, and the exact source meshes
are recorded in quick_fit_validation.json. The engine trials generated
supports and stayed within a 225 x 225 x 265 mm analysis envelope.
No live Cura frontend import or physical print was performed. Machine settings,
start/end G-code, Z offset and firmware calibration are inherited and unchanged.
No heat-set inserts are used in the current robot design.

After all relevant fits pass, continue with the R5 revision specimens and the
supported full-coxa/leg tests. These short jobs do not qualify printed strength,
warm creep, servo calibration or hardware walking.
