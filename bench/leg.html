Printed reference: yaw centred, femur horizontal/outward, tibia vertical down, 40 mm driver and follower vertical up, and 110 mm coupler parallel to the femur. In the logical model this is q1 = 0°, q2 = +90°, q3 = -90° and knee-driver angle = 0°.
To find each direction, use the Servo Bench for one small 16-32 tick jog. Positive roll moves toward +Y, positive hip moves the leg outward in +X, and positive knee-driver moves the tibia's absolute angle outward. Return to the printed reference after every jog.
Endpoint A and B are physical safe ends, not “up = upper” and “down = lower”. Capture them in either order; the page sorts their tick numbers automatically. Stop before hard contact and leave clearance for flex and bearing play.
The knee equation is exact only if both proximal servo axes are coaxial, driver = follower centre distance, coupler = femur centre distance, and all pivot axes are parallel. Measure those four conditions before powered coordinated motion.
Raw ranges alone cannot prevent four-bar collisions. Fusion cleared relative input from -30° to +40°; first commissioning keeps a 5° margin, so the page enforces -25° to +35°, equivalent to q3 from -115° to -55°.
