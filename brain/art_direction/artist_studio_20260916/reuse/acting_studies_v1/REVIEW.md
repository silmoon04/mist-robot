# Acting study review

Approved for the key-pose comparison. I inspected all six sheets, their 54 poses
and the combined overview, then compared the 18 examples with their manifest
labels and context notes. Exact manifest, overview and source hashes are recorded
in `visual_review.json`. No clipped faces or neighboring-cell artwork was seen.

The strongest sequences are Bluey's concern-to-reassurance and shared delight,
Inside Out's recall and careful checking, and the Aardman-inspired blink into
quiet realisation. They communicate thought changes through gaze and lids, with
small mouths. The Mickey-inspired set offers much larger comic accents. Simon's
Cat and Peanuts explore patient holds and smaller changes.

The limits matter when choosing examples:

- Aardman's relaxed listening settle can look bored if it lasts too long.
- Bluey's uncertainty remains puzzled or skeptical. Its tentative label fits.
- Inside Out reuses similar neutral poses; gaze direction in the middle beat
  carries much of the distinction between recall and checking.
- Mickey's double-take ends dubious. Mock outrage looks angry without the wink
  that follows it. Tiny triumph has a large accent, so its modest use depends on
  duration and context.
- Simon's clarification holds almost the same drawing for its last two poses.
  Its inward-converging pupils can look mildly cross-eyed or comically puzzled.
  It is suited to a light misunderstanding, not ordinary listening.
- Peanuts' company and pride examples share most of their pose vocabulary.
  Their ending gaze and proposed timing distinguish related uses. The small
  marks need a phone-distance check.

The manifest notes describe these main emotional ambiguities fairly. The still
sequences provide useful choices; they do not establish a single best acting
style or a final mapping for every conversation state.

This is a storyboard review. It does not require in-between frames and does not
approve smooth playback, speech synchronization or live expression policy.
Generated scale, anchors and background tone vary slightly between poses. Those
details, plus blink paths, interruptions and transition timing, need work after
the user chooses a direction. The preview durations remain proposals.
