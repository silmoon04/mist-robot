MIST drawn animation study

Seven eye families and 7 mouth assets contain 164 selected pose entries.
Emotion families share safe rounded sounds and closures; selected pose entries can reuse source cels.
Artwork: ImageGen. Existing MIST contours supplied as references.
Source PNGs are unchanged. White background removal happens in the browser.

atlas.json contains full cell rectangles, ink bounds, padded crop bounds and pivots.
cx/cy are absolute sheet coordinates. registration.anchorX/anchorY are relative to the cell.
Eyes use the lower lid baseline; mouths use the upper mouth baseline.
Use one constant scale per sequence. Do not resize each frame to the same bounds.
The current renderer targets 15 fps. Blink poses have timed holds; the audio clock stays independent.
FV, LNT and TH use separately reviewed replacement cels when frame.file is present.
Multiply source offsets and dimensions by frame.sourceScale, default 1. Never draw the old sheet for those cels.
atlas.mouthFamilies maps all source faces to an explicit speaking-mouth family.
These are reusable family studies; they do not represent 40 independently drawn face animations.
The silent study demonstrates poses. Voice tests follow scheduled audio playback.
Energy-only samples show timing, not phoneme recognition.
