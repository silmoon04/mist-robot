Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a new portrait 1024 x 1536 concept sheet of TEN MIST robot faces, arranged in an exact invisible 2-column x 5-row grid. Each cell is 512 x 307.2 pixels. The attached image is an IDENTITY AND EMOTION REFERENCE ONLY; redesign how the curves are drawn. Do not trace it or merely change line thickness.

Art direction: DRY BRUSH. Express these minimal cyan faces through a handful of confident painted gestures, like a skilled calligrapher with a nearly dry flat brush. Eye curves become open, angular sweeping gestural forms with slightly uneven arcs, tapered flicks and confident turns, visibly different construction from mathematically circular C shapes. Bristle channels and small skipped negative spaces run WITHIN the cyan strokes. Show deliberate dry outer edges, not random grit scattered around the face. Paint mouths in the same medium. Material and silhouette must read distinctly even as thumbnails. 10–20 percent silhouette variation from the reference is encouraged. Medium-wide strong strokes remain legible. The result must look hand-painted rather than vectors with a texture filter.

Use matte cyan #58e2d8 paint on a perfectly flat dark #101c20 background. No glow, shadows, 3D, realistic lighting or background texture. Keep few strokes and plenty of negative space. No heads, noses, bodies, extra eyebrows, lettering, labels, dividers or decorative splashes. Identity is only two minimal eyes plus a mouth where specified, with no mouths added to the two mouthless faces.

Reading order left to right, top to bottom:
1 Calm: open friendly brush-loop eyes, calm straight brush mouth. Neutral, not smiling.
2 Listening: attentive open loop eyes, small vertical oval open mouth.
3 Curious: inquisitive open loop eyes with a little asymmetric tilt and small resting dot mouth.
4 Quiet thought: half-closed, narrow almost rectangular brush eyes and tiny resting dot mouth. Thoughtful, not angry.
5 Happy: open brush-loop eyes and wide open happy bowl grin.
6 Playful: narrow half-closed eyes and a gently tilted smirk.
7 Deadpan: TWO CLOSED SHALLOW ARCS ONLY. MOUTHLESS. Do not add a dot or line below.
8 Sleepy: two closed drooping arcs and a tall oval yawn.
9 Delighted: TWO inward squeeze/chevron eyes ONLY. MOUTHLESS. Do not add mouth or dot.
10 Sad: open drooping loop eyes, an unmistakable frown, one long tear on the outer right.

All ten face centers remain in the middle of their respective cells with substantial margin. Keep scale consistent. Face features take roughly 65 percent cell width and 50 percent cell height. Coherent brush language across every face; minimal, high quality, emotionally clear. The user rejected previous proposals because they were just different stroke weights. This must genuinely change drawing construction, contour character and material.
