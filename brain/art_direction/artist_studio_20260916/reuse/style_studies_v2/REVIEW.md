# Visual review

Approved for the static comparison page. I inspected all three generated sheets
and the final comparison of ten expressions against the originals. The exact
manifest, comparison and source hashes are recorded in `visual_review.json`.

V1 held the original contours too closely. This set changes the drawings:

- Gesture ink uses tilted uneven loops, tapered endings, asymmetric eyes and
  hollow mouth shapes. It looks calligraphic, which is why the label no longer
  promises a loose pencil sketch.
- Dry brush uses painted sweeps, bristle gaps, broken openings and pointed
  endings. It keeps more of the original eye construction, while changing the
  silhouettes and how the marks are drawn.
- Cartoon cel uses organic filled shapes, bean-like eye openings, solid lids,
  upward-arched delighted eyes and a filled teardrop.

The three directions remain distinguishable in the small comparison tiles.
Quiet thought's corrected pen and cel lids no longer form the earlier angry
inward slope. Deadpan and Delighted remain mouthless. Sad keeps its frown and
single tear. I saw no clipped artwork or neighboring-cell content.

Some interpretation changes remain. Cartoon cel Calm has a small friendly smile
instead of a straight mouth. Quiet thought can still read as tired without
movement or context, and Deadpan retains the original restful ambiguity.
These are reasonable choices to compare, rather than proof that every emotion
will be read the same way by every person.

This approval covers static artwork only. These new styles have no tested
blinks, transitions, speech mouths or voice synchronization. The brush detail
also needs a small-screen motion check before use on the phone. Asset counts and
pixel checks were considered separately from the visual judgment.
