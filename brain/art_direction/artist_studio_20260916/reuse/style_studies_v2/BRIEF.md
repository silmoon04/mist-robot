# Drawing style revision

The first study changed stroke weight while locking the original contours too
tightly. The user rejected it as thickness variations rather than drawing styles.

This revision uses the same ten expressions and cyan-on-dark palette, while
allowing the artist to redraw the contours, negative spaces and gestures.

- Gesture ink: irregular ellipses, tilted loops and tapered line endings.
- Dry brush: painted sweeps, visible bristles, dry gaps and tapered flicks.
- Cartoon cel: organic bean shapes, crescent lids and solid expressive silhouettes.

Each direction must look different at thumbnail size, even if its stroke weight
were normalized. Merely changing thickness or placing a texture over unchanged
paths fails the brief. Preserve the emotional role, two-eye composition and
intentional absence of mouths on Deadpan and Delighted. Keep Sad's frown and tear.

Generate original raster artwork with built-in ImageGen. Keep each untouched
source and exact prompt. Export ten crops per direction, with the originals
alongside them. These are static proposals; do not change live assets or claim
that the variants are ready to blink or speak.
