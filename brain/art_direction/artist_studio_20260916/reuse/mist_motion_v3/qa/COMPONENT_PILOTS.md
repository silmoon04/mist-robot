# Component pilot review

Independent visual inspection of `components/eyes/round/contact_v1.png` and `components/eyes/hook/contact_v1.png`, before the composed acting clips exist. Frames below are one-based, row-major. These are still-sheet observations, not timed-playback acceptance.

## Round v1

The open C shape and downward closure stay in one contour family. There is no pupil, cap or inverted happy arc. This is substantially closer to the original MIST reference than v2's full-face sheets.

Frame 4 has two separate rounded left endpoints approaching one another. Frame 5 joins them with a visibly doubled cap or nub at the left junction; this needs a cleaner silhouette. Frame 6 is a single lower arc and a plausible closed endpoint. Final approval depends on the repaired near-closed junction and on the frame registration/timing in the composed face.

## Hook v1

The upper edge progressively descends and keeps the hook identity. However, frame 6 still contains a clearly visible horizontal aperture. It cannot be used as the closed frame of a blink.

The hook's stroke looks much heavier than the round component at comparable eye width: roughly sixty pixels versus forty in these sheets. Those are visual estimates, not an extraction measurement. The original MIST angry hook and neutral C eye use much closer stroke weights. A shared width fit alone would preserve this unwanted style difference. Keep the intended hook proportions but match the family weight more closely.

Both findings were sent directly to the eye-component agent and root before selecting final assets.
