# Independent visual review of MIST motion v3

Decision: acceptable as the corrected review gallery, with the limitations below. The malformed middle-pose defects seen in v2 were not found in the replacement acting contacts. This is not a claim that every loop has been watched continuously at production speed or that the acting is production-final.

Reviewed manifest: `12fe6c884de50dc303c1425d4f6e581104ce5e42d3d7e9639dd895ad41426533`.

## Evidence and limits

- Directly inspected original MIST neutral39, angry01, happy09 and sad26 faces.
- Directly inspected all eighteen twelve-pose dark contact sheets, reading every adjacent pair and each 12-to-1 seam. Re-inspected Waiting and Mock outrage after the final changes.
- Inspected the round/hook component pilots, final component sheet, and all three mouth-progressions.
- Composited existing blink frames into four diagnostic strips under `qa/blink_*.png`, covering every used left/right family combination: round/round, hook/hook, round/hook and hook/round. These are existing-frame review sheets, not altered artwork.
- Opened the local gallery, used the inspector, stepped blink controls and sampled a playing round blink at Actual and half speed. The browser showed closed, reopening and open poses with a stationary mouth and stable eye attachment positions. The inspector showed the intended timing controls and exposure labels.
- Browser tools return screenshots rather than a continuous visual stream. The sampled playback confirms rendered states, but cannot honestly establish the subjective smoothness of all eighteen clips at actual speed. The full adjacency review is stronger evidence about silhouette continuity than the limited playback samples are about timing feel. No audio/voice-sync judgment was made.

## Style and frame continuity

The canonical open C eye, rounded hook and small cyan mouth now remain recognizably MIST. Acting poses do not introduce pupils, filled eyeballs, detached brows, caps, bell-shaped eyes, teeth or tongue. The same eye contour persists within each acting clip; movement comes from small placement and angle changes. The mouth follows a single shape family through ordered in-betweens. Opening the mouth no longer visibly moves both eyes.

The acting contacts no longer contain the v2 bar-dot-smile-bar sequences, happy-arc blink inversions, or one-frame wholesale eye-model substitutions. The return paths are compatible with their entries, and the loop endpoints match visually.

The new blinks are separate from the acting beat. They close downward through the same contour family and share a fully closed lower arc. Mixed eye-family pairs retain their identity on reopening. The round third component does widen slightly; at composed size this is a small contour wobble, not a pupil/eye-model swap. It remains the main polish caveat for the blink. No further blind regeneration is warranted before the user sees the loop.

## Changes requested and rechecked

1. The original round pilot had a doubled left junction near closure. The final selection substantially reduced that visual nub.
2. The original hook pilot never fully closed and was too heavy relative to the round eye. Final components use a true closed contour and closer stroke weight.
3. Asking, then waiting originally grew a smile at the long waiting hold. The final version now gives an early small smile, then settles to a flat mouth across the late waiting poses. Rechecked its twelve-pose contact.
4. Mock outrage originally combined hooked eyes with a frown, making the supposed joke read as straightforward anger. The final version uses a small smile with the eye squeeze. Rechecked its twelve-pose contact; the playful reading is now more plausible.

## Remaining scope

This correction prioritizes consistent MIST identity and continuous component motion. Several clips deliberately share the same eye/mouth families; they are not eighteen independently redesigned characters. Their degree of subtlety and comic exaggeration still benefits from the user's side-by-side selection. Transitions and timing arithmetic require the separate runtime tests; this report does not turn those technical checks into an aesthetic verdict. Live voice synchronization is outside this review gallery.
