# Reference-led face study

The user wants stronger stylisation like the supplied thinking-face illustration.
The earlier studies changed line weight and drawing material but kept too much
of the original geometry. This study uses the supplied image directly.

Two directions use the same ten emotional roles:

- Reference style follows the illustrated lids, gaze and rounded luminous marks.
- More expressive pushes eye asymmetry, squash and facial poses further.

The reference's half-lidded eyes have a substantial upper edge joined to a round
lower cup. Some faces combine an open eye with a closed arc or lowered lid.
The planning face uses filled eye forms with dark cutouts that establish gaze.
These are the useful differences to develop. Thickness, texture and glow alone
do not satisfy this brief.

Faces must communicate without the activity icons. Listening has a small closed
mouth. Quiet thought should look attentive or contemplative, not angry. Deadpan
and Delighted remain mouthless; Sad retains one tear and a frown. Eye shapes can
change freely while preserving a simple two-eye MIST character.

These are static concept images. They do not replace the live animation assets.
Source sheets and exact prompts remain available; individual face images are
rectangular crops of the generated sheets.
