:::IMPORTANT ANNOUNCEMENT:::

Dear players, readers, mushroom pickers, robots and random visitors,

Today we announce that we'll soon be closing the official Amanita Design forum.

First of all, massive shout out to Alex, Lamkin and Dmitriy who worked so hard for so many years to keep you up to date with all things Amanita and kindly offered help to everyone who experienced any issues with our games. We could never thank you enough guys:)

The forum will be closed on May 1st, 2018. It won't be deleted entirely, but it will become impossible to create new topics or reply to old ones.

For updates on our games, follow us on Facebook and Twitter!

If you're in need of help and you haven't found a solution here, please contact support@amanita-design.net or use the Steam forums:
CHUCHEL
Samorost 3
Botanicula
Machinarium
Samorost 2

Thank you for understanding and the ongoing support,
Lukas
