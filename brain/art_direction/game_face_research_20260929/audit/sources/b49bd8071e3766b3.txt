Chicory: A Colorful Tale Steam PS Store OST Merch Discord A Painting Adventure You're a dog wielding a magic paintbrush! Use the power of art to explore, solve puzzles, help your animal friends and restore color to the world in this top-down adventure game. Explore the Picnic Province, and draw on anything! Manipulate the environment with your paint and solve puzzles! Unlock new paint abilities and use them to reach new places! Local co-op! Play with your friends and paint together! Along the way you'll meet many characters and make friends. Collect clothes, furniture and brush styles to unleash your creativity and dress yourself and the world as you see fit! ★ Follow Future Development ★ Chicory: A Colorful Tale (Original Soundtrack) by Lena Raine A Catchy, Playful Soundtrack Composed by Lena Raine, the acclaimed composer for titles like Celeste and Guild Wars , the Chicory soundtrack mixes classical-sounding instruments with modern-feeling structure.
