      ANTONBLAST is a fast-paced explosive action platformer that's all about destruction, available for the first time in physical form for the Nintendo Switch™!

      This physical edition includes:

      • A retro-inspired operation card to reference as you master the game

      • Two kiss-cut sticker sheets
      • Original reversible cover art
      • A digital download code for a copy of the complete soundtrack

That's right: Satan himself has stolen Anton's prized Spirit collection, and he'll stop at nothing to get it back. Of course, you have to help him.

      Your mission in every level is simple: SET the Detonators, FIND Anton's Spirit, and ESCAPE before Happy Hour ends!
