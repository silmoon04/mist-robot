NIGHT IN THE WOODS

And there's something in the woods.

OVERVIEW

College dropout Mae Borowski returns home to the crumbling former mining town of Possum Springs seeking to resume her aimless former life and reconnect with the friends she left behind. But things aren't the same. Home seems different now and her friends have grown and changed. Leaves are falling and the wind is growing colder. Strange things are happening as the light fades.

And there's something in the woods.

Night in the Woods is an adventure game focused on exploration, story, and character, featuring dozens of characters to meet and lots to do across a lush, vibrant world. After a successful Kickstarter it's being made by Infinite Fall, a teamup of Alec Holowka (Aquaria), Scott Benson (Late Night Work Club), and Bethany Hockenberry.

ABOUT THE GAME

Night in the Woods is a 2D adventure game that progresses through conversations with the various characters in Possum Springs.Run around the town and talk with the various characters. The conversation content is quite diverse. You may get lost in conversation without the correct answers, or you may stumble into a surprise confession about some serious troubles. Any choice you make is the same as life, with no game over. Please enjoy the realistic developments after selecting your answer. There are various mini games also available between the game story. Practice in a band, break cars and more throughout the game as you play the role of Mae.

WHAT IS NIGHT IN THE WOODS?

Night In The Woods is an adventure game developed by Infinite Fall and sold globally by Finji, already known for their multiple masterpieces. The game, which creates a rather unique view of the world, with realistic human emotions, creative stories and distinctive features. The game won the Grand Prize at the Independent Games Festival 2018, and has since won numerous awards, clearly highlighting the title as a true indie adventure game masterpiece.
