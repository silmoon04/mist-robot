About This Game

Wirm is a delightful physics climbing game about being a worm on a tiny adventure.

Use your soft squishy body to wiggle over obstacles.

Monch your way through the dirt, and find hidden objects underground.

Bring your friends and wiggle together in local multiplayer, or online through Steam's Remote Play Together!

come with me and watch me squirm

i would eat through dirt all day

bring your friends — they'll be wirms too!
