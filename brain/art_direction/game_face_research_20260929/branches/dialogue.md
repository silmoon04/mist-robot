# Dialogue portraits: research notes

The three selected references answer different questions. In Stars and Time is the strongest immediate moodboard choice for mixed feelings in grayscale. UNDERTALE supplies a readable sequence in which the face and words can disagree. OMORI supplies a restrained hold with small changes in the drawing. The original media URLs and individual credit qualifications are in `dialogue.json`.

## In Stars and Time: begin with Odile

The [official screenshot](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvMTM5MDUxMS8xMzYzNTA1MC5wbmc=/original/hnB32k.png) is unusually useful for MIST. Odile's partly closed eyes and pinched inner brow remain tired while the mouth offers a small, uneven smile. The line is superficially reassuring and darkly humorous. This does not reduce cleanly to a happy or sad preset. The artist has put different information into the upper and lower face.

The [battle screenshot](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvMTM5MDUxMS8xMzYzNTA2MS5wbmc=/original/NbpSwM.png) gives a second comparison: a smirk, worry, a forceful attack and fatigue remain distinct in monochrome. The different eye openings and mouth shapes matter more than decorative detail. Both images came from the [official itch page](https://armor-games-studios.itch.io/in-stars-and-time), and both were visually inspected.

[Adrienne Bazir's own biography](https://www.insertdisc5.com/about) identifies insertdisc5 as a game developer and cartoonist with 2D animation experience. [The publisher](https://armorgamesstudios.com/games/in-stars-and-time) names insertdisc5 as the developer. These sources do not isolate who animated each portrait, so the board must not invent that narrower credit.

Suggested MIST experiment, our inference: construct one expression with contradictory but compatible parts, such as a reassuring mouth and uncertain lids. Hold it through one thought, change it at the qualifying clause, and let it recover after the reply. These are proposed cues, not measured ISAT timings.

## UNDERTALE: the change of intention is the cue

The [official Flowey Alarm Clock dialogue](https://undertale.com/alarmclock/flowey/) is a sequence of published still panels. Several were inspected in the browser. The flower silhouette and position remain stable while the eyes and mouth change. During the bicycle exchange, an open-eyed smile accompanies a very specific description of a supposedly unwanted gift. That juxtaposition carries the mixed feeling. Later, isolated pauses interrupt the account of remembered kindness.

This is a useful reference for staging a sequence: social expression, defensive explanation, revealing detail, denial. It is not evidence for any specific number of frames or milliseconds. The web page lets the reader supply the hold duration.

Credit needs care. [Temmie's interview with Toby](https://toby.fangamer.com/interviews/temmie/) records her reaction to Flowey's acting in an early demo. It does not say she authored the selected Flowey portraits. [The official Chapter 2 update](https://undertale.com/deltarune-update-092021/) explicitly credits Temmie as DELTARUNE's Main Artist and Main Animator, and Toby describes designing and writing the game. Keep those claims separate. Do not label all UNDERTALE faces as Temmie animation.

Suggested MIST experiment, our inference: let a small smile survive a moment of uncertainty while the eyes change first. Put a held pause before the admission, and change the mouth only when the character drops the social expression. Speech articulation should not erase the emotional pose.

## OMORI: a hold can still look alive

[OMOCAT's portfolio](https://www.omocat.com/omori) supplies the [neutral portrait GIF](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/61d61babf89b4d299bb8ea6b/bbde21ae-c89f-45a3-afc4-8eeb9e2f557f/tumblr_oj3c4gMAW71qegob8o7_r1_640.gif). Its three stored frames were decoded and inspected individually. The dark oval eyes and tiny mouth retain the same expression while contours, hair and shoulders change a little. This is a concrete example of drawing variation during an emotional hold. Continuous playback was not perceived, so motion verification remains false in the data.

[The artist CV](https://www.omocat.com/pagecv) lists OMORI illustration and animation/video-editing as separate roles. That verifies animation involvement without assigning every game asset to one person. The portfolio collection is broader than final game assets; the selected GIF should be described as a creator-supplied portfolio reference.

Suggested MIST experiment, our inference: make listening a strong, deliberately held pose. If it needs visual life, add subtle material variation without constantly altering emotional features. A robot face might use a stable display texture instead of pencil contours. The restraint matters more than the texture.

## Additional leads and limits

- DELTARUNE's [official Chapter 2 concept commentary](https://toby.fangamer.com/interviews/chapter2_concept/) contains a particularly relevant creator statement: Queen became more humorous as Toby wrote her dialogue, and he found the initial straight-on sprite lacking in personality. This supports designing the face and dialogue together. It is not a measured animation example, so it is supporting reading rather than a fourth moodboard card.
- OneShot's [official developer itch page](https://futurecatgames.itch.io/oneshot) and Nightmargin's creator profile were found. No precise primary-source portrait/animation credit and strong inspected portrait image were secured in this branch, so OneShot was not promoted into a fully evidenced card.
- OMORI emotion charts are easy to find on fan wikis and fan-art pages. This pass deliberately uses creator-hosted media; it does not quietly substitute a fan-made expression sheet.
- The official ISAT trailer embed was verified from the publisher page. It was not watched continuously, and no timestamps are claimed.
- Browser navigation to itch.io repeatedly timed out. Official screenshot URLs were read from the publisher-owned page's HTML and the original images inspected separately. Only original URLs belong in the moodboard; temporary inspection files are not deliverables.

No existing MIST art or application files were changed.
