# Narrative animation studios and expressive indie faces

This branch adds three visual references and one production reference. The most useful starting point for MIST is **Later Alligator**, followed by the official **Smile For Me** response comparisons.

The GIF URLs below are supplied by the linked sources. Later Alligator, Smile For Me and Great God Grove images returned HTTP 200 with image/gif content types and were inspected in the browser. Inspection was through sampled screenshots, not continuous playback. No frame counts, blink timing or transition durations are claimed.

## Later Alligator: Sweet Geraldine

[Creator interview and supplied GIF](https://community.wacom.com/en-gb/later-alligator-a-qa-with-the-small-buteras/)

![Sweet Geraldine dialogue pose, SmallBu / Pillow Fight](https://community.wacom.com/en-us/us/wp-content/uploads/sites/40/2019/10/lateralligator.gif)

The inspected frame gives the eyes most of the space and lets a small mouth supply attitude. This is useful for MIST because personality can come from the relationship between eye direction and mouth shape before adding more motion.

**Credits:** Lindsay Small-Butera describes directing, writing and animating; Alex Small-Butera is the keyframer and also animates. Exact roles and production observations are recorded in the JSON. The interview establishes joint authorship, not the animator of this individual shot.

**Our proposed test:** confident, sheepish and uncertain faces with the same base proportions. Each should read before it moves.

## Smile For Me: answering another character

[Official game page](https://limbolane.com/games/smile-for-me) and [current press kit](https://limbolane.com/games/smile-for-me/press).

![Putunia nod/shake comparison, LimboLane](https://images.ctfassets.net/uhpvzhjs1ddl/33Zq1xMfT58jEYUx2rTSvd/334f2558cd1e6e0cc4618b8861d404f1/SFM_GIF_Nod_Shake_Comparison_Putunia.gif)

![Jerafina nod/shake comparison, LimboLane](https://images.ctfassets.net/uhpvzhjs1ddl/2hBB5VqN7IvFIPK9sxqBru/b6b44e65225c9465e26d5975516d21be/SFM_GIF_Nod_Shake_Comparison_Jerafina.gif)

Yugo Limbo is a verified animator and co-creator; Day Lane is a co-creator, producer, programmer and gameplay designer. Do not relabel Day as an animator, or assign every character drawing solely to Yugo.

The useful comparison is conversational attitude. Putunia's eyes, mouth and glove form a confrontational pose; Jerafina's tilted head and conspicuous lips form a relaxed one. These are sampled poses, not a verified description of the full response animations.

**Our proposed test:** MIST acknowledges or declines an input with a specific attentive or hesitant pose. This makes the response feel like part of a conversation.

## Great God Grove: eye and mouth contrast

[Official press kit and animation credit](https://limbolane.com/games/great-god-grove/press).

![God dialogue face comparison, LimboLane](https://images.ctfassets.net/uhpvzhjs1ddl/5JfVe7RAVhpR7FAH50Jfle/0084285294749e67f64ad916b5dca0d1/storepage_gif3_nosplit.gif)

The inspected frame pairs closed angled eyelids with star-shaped eyes on the other face. Mouths remain relatively compact. It is a useful option if MIST needs a stronger expression vocabulary without a much larger mouth.

The press kit credits **Jack Cornish for 3d animations**. It does not identify the animator of this individual shot.

**Our proposed test:** make an inward-looking closed-eye expression visibly distinct from a routine blink.

## Thank Goodness You're Here!: preserving the drawn pose

[Pip Williamson's creator portfolio](https://www.williamsonpip.co.uk/project/thank-goodness-youre-here) and [the portfolio's linked behind-the-scenes film](https://www.youtube.com/embed/zOCLmbFMeL8?start=248).

Pip identifies herself as animation team lead. Her account explains why free-drawn roughs and replacement sprites were part of the engine workflow. This is a production reference, with no researcher-verified face or motion claims.

**Our proposed test:** draw the mouth and eyelid extremes, then decide which need replacement shapes. Compare the implementation to those drawings before judging how smooth it is.

The portfolio supplies a GIF and storyboards. Direct inspection of its GIF was blocked by browser site-safety policy, so it is excluded from display media. The video start position comes from the portfolio embed and is not a verified research timestamp.
