# Stylized 3D game faces: evidence-bound research

The strongest immediate visual is Slime Rancher's six-face GIF. Lil Gator adds a well-documented head/body timing method. Miyuki Richardson's Psychonauts 2 reel provides an attributed robot comparison. Bugsnax remains a useful artist lead with a blocked viewer, and should be shown as a follow-up rather than a verified face study.

## 1. Slime Rancher: small face vocabulary

[Official six-face GIF](https://steamcdn-a.akamaihd.net/steamcommunity/public/images/clans/11544764/dbcc1e5c037a11b9f2583407bb057fc247464ac2.gif)

[Developer post, 25 June 2019](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=433340&enddate=1576525914&feed=steam_community_announcements)

Ian McConville introduces himself as Monomi Park's Principal Artist (spelled “Principle” in the post) and describes his rebuild of the face shaders. That supports a face-system author credit; it does not justify inventing an animator credit.

The sampled GIF frame is valuable even before timing analysis. The bottom-centre smile uses dot eyes and a broad mouth. The bottom-right worried face keeps tiny eyes but changes the mouth to a small downturned shape. The startled bottom-left face enlarges the eye marks while keeping the mouth compact. The same pink body supports all of them.

The implementation explanation is concrete: separate eye and mouth mask channels, distance fields, adjustable mouth thresholds and UV distortion. These are graphic faces on 3D bodies, not evidence of a blendshape-based acting pipeline.

**MIST proposal, our inference:** author a few distinct eye/mouth combinations first. Allow eye direction, eye opening and mouth state to change independently. A delayed mouth response is worth testing, but was not measured in this GIF. Keep wobble secondary to a readable pose.

Verification: official GIF extracted from the rendered post, loaded and visually inspected as a frame. Continuous motion was not perceived. No blink or hold durations are asserted.

## 2. Lil Gator Game: head weight and restrained follow-through

[Scott Slucher developer interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-exaggerated-animations-and-tottering-wobble-bones-of-lil-gator-game)

[Inspected sword-swing still](https://eu-images.contentstack.com/v3/assets/blt740a130ae3c5d529/blt99c954c98b135471/650effa9c6abf427f547e08d/Gator_3.jpg/?width=1400&auto=webp&quality=80&disable=upscale)

The useful evidence is Slucher's own account of how he designed and animated the character. Large head and arm proportions amplify small actions. His animation system blends movement according to speed and surface angle; head lag and turning lean sit above that. He explicitly says the smaller blended movements mean the character is not always making large gestures.

The inspected still makes the face readable through a large pale eye, black pupil, open jaw and broad head silhouette even amid flying cardboard. It does not establish facial timing.

**MIST proposal, our inference:** a modest head follow-through can make attention shifts feel physical. Separate a quiet glance from an enthusiastic whole-body gesture. Return to a settled pose instead of preserving spring motion indefinitely.

Verification: first-person creator interview and supplied still inspected. The historical Gfycat URL in the article is https://gfycat.com/ifr/ZigzagAshamedBarnowl, but it is not being offered as verified working motion. No claim about Lil Gator's facial textures or blendshapes is supported here.

## 3. Psychonauts 2: a robot's attention and task

[Miyuki Richardson's own reel](https://vimeo.com/619494977)

[Embed returned by the player's Embed dialog](https://player.vimeo.com/video/619494977?h=831ae4368a)

[Official Double Fine credits](https://www.doublefine.com/games/psychonauts-2/credits)

[Artist resume](https://maruyamamiyuki.wixsite.com/portfolio/resume)

The mailroom robot sequence around 4:08 to 4:20 is the useful MIST comparison. The sampled 4:14 frame brings the circular luminous eye and the envelope into the same composition; the large mechanical hand carries the task. The sampled 4:20 frame visibly labels the work “Layout and Animation.”

Preserve the reel's attribution boundaries. At 1:36, the inspected shot says “Layout Only” and credits final animation to Squeeze Animation Studios. Richardson is officially credited as an animator, but not every finished performance in her reel is her animation. Other sections also label storyboard contributions.

**MIST proposal, our inference:** attach a gaze/head/body reaction to a specific stimulus. Mechanical features can retain their design while orientation and body gestures supply emphasis. For our own studies, label anticipation, attention shift, hold and return so we can judge the performance instead of adding motion everywhere.

Verification: player playback and timestamps worked, exact embed URL was read from its dialog, and selected frames were inspected. Continuous acting, blink timing and mouth delay were not perceived or measured. The official credits acknowledge FaceFX, but that does not establish a particular shot's facial mechanism. The reel contains major story spoilers. Double Fine is a larger-production comparison here.
