# Cozy and character-led face references

Four studies across three games. The most useful MIST references are the Dicey Dungeons dialogue GIF and the Spiritfarer trailer close-up. Both were visually inspected. All video and GIF records retain `motionVerified: false`: this pass inspected displayed frames, not continuous movement.

## 1. Dicey Dungeons: composed attention versus excitement

[Marlowe Dobbe's official credit](https://diceydungeons.com/credits.html) explicitly covers art and animation. Her [portfolio](https://marlowedobbe.com/dicey-dungeons) confirms the work.

![Lady Luck and the warrior](https://terrycavanaghgames.com/dice/presskit/xbox/dicey_dungeons/images/amdicenow.gif)

Credit: Marlowe Dobbe / Dicey Dungeons team. [Official press-kit source](https://terrycavanaghgames.com/dice/presskit/xbox/).

Lady Luck's half-lidded sideways look and small smile contrast with the warrior's raised brows, open eyes and grin. This is useful for distinguishing mild amusement from excitement. The supplied GIF is actual dialogue imagery, not cover art.

**Our MIST inference:** let eye direction and lid height carry the listening attitude while mouth shapes change. Reserve wider eyes for a meaningful increase in attention. The source does not establish a particular lip-sync implementation.

## 2. Spiritfarer: a production method for individual reactions

The [developer press kit](https://thunderlotusgames.com/press-kits/spiritfarer-press-kit/) credits animation director Alexandre Boyer and animators Simon Nakauchi Pelletier and Mathieu Choquet. Boyer and Pelletier discuss their work in [Toon Boom's first-person interview](https://www.toonboom.com/thunder-lotus-games-on-animating-the-afterlife-in-spiritfarer).

[Official in-game launch trailer](https://www.youtube.com/embed/Xu4JHmcfrtw)

**Our MIST inference:** a shared reaction structure can still have distinct gaze, eyelids and recovery for each emotion. Give a reaction enough time to read, then allow an incoming conversation event to interrupt its ending.

This entry is process-led. The official fishing still inspected was too wide to support facial observations, so it is excluded from the moodboard media.

## 3. Spiritfarer animated trailer: modest warmth

![Stella holding Daffodil](https://www.toonboom.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Spiritfarer_Animated_Trailer_Screenshot_04-1024x576.jpg)

Credit: Knights of the Light Table / Thunder Lotus. [Source and animation credits](https://www.toonboom.com/spiritfarer-animated-trailer). [Official animated trailer](https://www.youtube.com/embed/PZfqGU6Y5uU).

Stella's upward gaze and small closed smile make a strong reference for quiet affection. Her embrace supports the face without requiring a big grin.

David Liu directed and animated the trailer; the source names six further animators, preserved in the JSON. These are trailer credits, separate from the game's team. No individual shot attribution was found.

**Our MIST inference:** try a small mouth curve and attentive upward gaze for contentment. Use this as a pose reference, not evidence of blink timing or speech animation.

## 4. Cult of the Lamb: graphic reaction contrast

[Studio-published reaction clip](https://i.giphy.com/kjua05Qw2EXPJ4NlvR.mp4) · [GIPHY source](https://giphy.com/gifs/cult-of-the-lamb-massive-monster-kjua05Qw2EXPJ4NlvR)

Two inspected frames show the rabbit's large dark oval eyes with pale vertical pupils, while a small tooth-bearing mouth changes to an open smile. A foreground pointing finger supplies interaction context. The sampled frames cannot establish the exact timing or causal sequence.

[Massive Monster's staff page](https://massivemonster.com/about) credits James Pearmain as Art Director and Jan F Sorrentino as Animator. It also identifies Jan's Doom & Shroom as fan-animation. Neither credit identifies who animated this particular clip.

**Our MIST inference:** hold the defining eye design through small mouth changes and direct attention toward an event. This reference is less suitable for restrained warmth than the first and third studies.

## Gaps and rejected leads

- Ooblets was explored through official devlogs, but the material located did not provide a sufficiently specific facial study and animator attribution for this branch.
- TOEM was not developed into a sourced entry.
- Boredom, nervousness, asymmetric eye behavior and emotion through continuous speech remain under-evidenced here. Half-lidded Lady Luck is an attitude reference; calling it boredom would overstate the frame.
- No external videos were downloaded and no replacement GIFs were manufactured.
- Media URLs came from live first-party HTML or the rendered studio player. The browser inspected the Dicey GIF frame, both supplied Spiritfarer trailer stills, and two Cult of the Lamb clip frames. All continuous-motion conclusions remain open.
