# Exaggerated indie faces

Three references survived the source and visual checks. The most useful animator-specific discovery is **Juniper / PinPan**, who identifies herself as ANTONBLAST's lead gameplay animator. The most useful process account is **Derek Lieu's BattleBlock Theater cutscene breakdown**, which separates his animation work from Dan Paladin's art and earlier animation. Pizza Tower supplies a strong shape-language comparison through its creator's interview.

The JSON companion contains the exact media URLs, credit scopes and evidence limits. All `motionVerified` values are false: screenshots of loaded media were inspected, but continuous motion was not perceived. No invented filmstrip, frame count or measured hold is included.

## Pizza Tower / Pizza Tower Guy (McPig)

[Creator interview and artwork](https://gamingreinvented.com/interview/an-interview-with-the-developer-of-wario-land-successor-pizza-tower/)

![Peppino expressive poses](https://gamingreinvented.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/PeppinoArtwork-1024x683.jpg)

The illustration is worth putting directly on the board. Eye whites spill outside the head mass. Mouth area varies dramatically. The face points toward the action instead of remaining centered in the same construction. These are visible differences between drawings, not evidence of their order in an animation.

For MIST, the useful question is how much its digital marks can change shape while its housing remains recognisable. Try three plainly different reaction drawings first. If they cannot be distinguished as thumbnails, more interpolation will not solve the underlying problem. Avoid borrowing Peppino's nose, teeth or anxious chef identity.

**Unverified:** anticipation, smear placement, held-peak duration and recovery timing. The 2019 interview supports the creator's animation role at that time, not a full final-release staff list.

## ANTONBLAST / Juniper, also known as PinPan

[Animator's first-person profile](https://pinpan.itch.io/) · [Official page containing the close-up](https://www.fangamer.com/collections/popular/products/antonblast-game-nintendo-switch) · [Direct reaction clip](https://www.fangamer.com/cdn/shop/videos/c/vp/2736a2cbb0124e29bc1287cb906bcd57/2736a2cbb0124e29bc1287cb906bcd57.SD-480p-0.9Mbps-50188450.mp4?v=0)

The clip is substantially more relevant than a trailer because the face fills the image. Browser inspection showed an open-mouth pose near its beginning and a clenched-teeth pose at its end. Narrow eyes and the very large white tooth mass make the latter read clearly. The broad change in mouth area is a stronger reference than a generic instruction to add squash and stretch.

The proposed MIST study should use a clear event: unexpected information arrives, MIST reacts, then regains composure. Draw those three meanings distinctly, hold the reaction long enough to read, and let the final pose retain a little emotional residue. Those are proposed choices for MIST; they are not measurements of this clip.

**Attribution boundary:** PinPan's profile supports lead gameplay animator. It does not establish authorship of this exact close-up. Do not label Tony Grayson as the animator merely because he leads the project. The indexed profile was readable, but direct navigation timed out. The official video loaded and played; only separate visible poses were inspected.

## BattleBlock Theater / Derek Lieu and Dan Paladin

[Official animator breakdown](https://blog.thebehemoth.com/2013/05/07/battleblock-theater-cutscene-development/) · [Final intro supplied by Derek Lieu](https://vimeo.com/65080296)

![BattleBlock expressive friends](https://blog.thebehemoth.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/08-shot-with-lightswm.jpg)

The simple faces are a useful counterweight to Peppino and Anton. MIST does not need additional anatomy to act. A small number of readable eye and mouth configurations can work if gaze, pose arrival and settling have intention.

For a prototype, make a plain black-and-white face study with no glow. Give a reaction one main change in direction and one recovery. Stagger the eyes and mouth only where the thought calls for it. Compare that against moving the entire face as one layer. This would test whether the current weakness comes from the acting or the rendering.

**Credit boundary:** the official article credits Paladin for the 2011 opening/prologue art and animation, and Lieu for the nine shipped cutscenes. The finished reference should therefore not be credited solely to Paladin. The article supplies evidence for motion-path sketching and controlled overshoot; it does not supply facial anticipation or hold measurements.

## Research limits and selection

- Castle Crashers was not added simply to increase the count. The BattleBlock source provides a more precise animator credit and directly relevant process evidence.
- Wario remains background context rather than a separate entry. This pass did not find a comparably specific credited animator source to justify a slot.
- The official ANTONBLAST publisher GIF is included in JSON as action context, but the close-up video should be the primary reference. Fangamer's `HAPPYHOUR.gif` was inspected and rejected because it only shows lettering.
- No source animation was downloaded, re-cut or represented by fabricated frames. Publicly supplied original media remain linked to their source pages.

Before choosing exact timing for MIST, watch the linked close-up and cutscene continuously and annotate anticipation, peak and recovery against the actual player. The present board can support selection of shape language and acting methods; it cannot support claims about production frame counts.
