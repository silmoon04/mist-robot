# Hand-drawn game faces: evidence and usable references

The strongest visual material in this branch is Tina Nawrocki's own Cuphead pencil work. These are original animator-posted drawings, not fan reconstructions. Three character studies give different useful lessons: conflicting attitudes inside a fixed shape, a mouth that changes the character's apparent intent, and a large theatrical reaction that preserves identity.

Fourteen original drawings were fetched and visually inspected. The JSON contains their exact remote URLs. The original posts also contain Blogger video embeds, but those clips were not continuously viewed. Do not label the still comparisons as verified timing, transition order, blink motivation or recovery. No videos were downloaded and no replacement GIFs were manufactured.

## Visual choices

### Pip and Dot
[Original animator post](https://tinanawrocki.blogspot.com/2017/10/cuphead-pip-and-dot-domino.html)

A particularly useful reference for MIST because the face sits inside a hard geometric design. The upper and lower faces disagree in the idle drawing: clenched teeth above, a grin below. Compare the hat drawings for lid and brow changes. The attack drawing is useful specifically because a large structural deformation preserves recognisable smaller faces; its combat function is incidental to this study.

Suggested MIST experiment, our inference: one asymmetric listening pose with a restrained mouth. Establish which visual anchors must survive before allowing a reaction to distort the face.

### Hopus Pocus
[Original animator post](https://tinanawrocki.blogspot.com/2017/10/cuphead-hopus-pocus-magician-rabbit.html)

Use the first and fourth supplied attack drawings as a mouth-shape comparison. The small two-tooth smile and predatory grin imply different intent. The smear is a drawing of coordinated deformation, not proof that independent eye scaling will produce good acting.

Suggested MIST experiment, our inference: three purpose-drawn mouth silhouettes. Judge them as static expressions before judging their transitions.

### Baroness Von Bon Bon
[Original animator post](https://tinanawrocki.blogspot.com/2017/09/cuphead-baroness-von-bon-bon.html)

The strongest face range in the set. Closed eyes and a small mouth contrast with an open jaw, clenched grin and startled eyes. Her hair and hat remain identifying landmarks. The detached head is an extreme identity test, not a literal recommendation for MIST.

Suggested MIST experiment, our inference: make a four-expression sheet and reject any expression that reads only when its label is visible.

## Process references

[Jake Clark's GDC talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RmGb-jU3uVQ) is a verified official YouTube embed: oEmbed returned the exact title and GDC channel. [Clark's own announcement](https://jake-clark.tumblr.com/post/158375449032/hey-everyone-heres-a-talk-i-recently-gave-at-gdc) establishes authorship. The talk was not continuously watched, so the board should present it as further viewing rather than attach invented timestamps.

[Klei's 2014 GDC session](https://gdcvault.com/play/1020165/2D-Animation-at-Klei), by Jeff Agala and Aaron Bouthillier, covers symbol animation and responsive implementation. Keep it in the process bibliography, not the face gallery. Its value to MIST is the possibility of combining reusable parts with deliberately redrawn extremes. That adaptation is our inference.

## Credit and retrieval boundaries

- Mariel Cartwright identifies herself as Skullgirls' lead animator in [On Animation in Skullgirls](https://skullgirls.com/2011/04/on-animation-in-skullgirls/). Her [Getting the Details Right](https://skullgirls.com/2011/05/animation-getting-the-details-right/) post also identifies animator Jon “Persona” Kim. Search indexing exposed these statements, but direct retrieval returned AccessDenied. No uninspected combat GIF was substituted for a face reference. The previously indexed GDC YouTube ID Mw0h9WmBIsw returned 404 from oEmbed and is omitted.
- Alex Ahad is not listed as the animator of any shot here. A design credit would not establish shot animation authorship.
- [Team Cherry's own biography](https://www.teamcherry.com.au/about) confirms Ari Gibson as co-director and former operator of Mechanical Apple. This alone does not establish authorship of a particular Hollow Knight facial shot. Hollow Knight is therefore omitted from the visual shortlist.
- Klei's talk is documented as GDC 2014 by the official record and developer forum. A later Game Developer article calls it a 2017 talk; use the primary record's date.

All MIST ideas above are proposed experiments. This branch changed only its own JSON and Markdown deliverables.
