Independent visual QA of MIST studies, groups 5–7

Reviewed 2026-09-29 against BRIEF.md and assignments_5.json through assignments_7.json. Each selected canonical sheet in the frozen group manifests was inspected with view_image at original full-sheet resolution. Only these QA reports were authored; the sheets, prompts, and UI were not edited.

All 15 selected sheets pass the required checks: exactly five complete faces in cells 1–5 of the invisible 3×2 grid; no facial artwork in cell 6; cyan MIST identity; two eyes and one mouth per face; no text, props, detached facial fragments, or fused features; five readable attitudes; and unsmiling skeptical and concerned mouths. All selected sheets are 1536×1024 PNGs, and each copy matches the manifest's returned generated original byte for byte.

Equal-cell pixel measurements support the full-sheet visual check. The smallest cyan-art margin is 30 pixels, and all 15 sixth cells contain zero detected cyan pixels. These measurements check artwork presence and cell boundaries; the visual review supplies face-count and emotion judgments.

All 15 descriptions and selected prompts pass the separate source-claim review. Process-only and uninspected-source studies are described as authored MIST experiments. No reviewed caption falsely attributes the generated faces to an observed game facial style or measured source timing.

Three sheets have a moderate study-differentiation weakness: Cuphead / Jake Clark, Klei, and Ori. They share much of the generic C-eye/open-arc construction and pose vocabulary. Their source principles differ mainly through process descriptions. This does not invalidate their distinct required attitudes, and no further repair is required by the rejection criteria. Minor similarities and subjective preferences are recorded per study below.

The dark backdrops and cyan marks retain faint tonal variation. This is visible but creates no framing, feature-count, or emotion failure. These are static studies. This review does not establish physical robot-screen legibility or animated acting.
