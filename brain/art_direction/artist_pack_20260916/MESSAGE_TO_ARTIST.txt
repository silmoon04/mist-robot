I'm working on a little robot with a phone for its face. I'd like it to feel a bit more hand-drawn: soft uneven lines, slight asymmetry and a few expressive squishes, but still clean and nice to look at. It shouldn't be constantly bouncing around.

I put together references and a proposed drawing list so this isn't a vague request to animate loads of faces. The main things to get right are its everyday face, listening, thinking with a closed mouth, and how those look while talking.

Could we start with two small style directions, each showing neutral, happy and thinking? Once we pick one, the first animation test only needs six reusable components. The proposed full set is 38 component drawings, mostly eye pairs and separate mouths. That includes blink shapes and a few extra gesture drawings. It doesn't mean 38 full animations or hundreds of new frames; we can reuse the pieces and animate a lot of the movement in code.

The exact timing and amount of roughness aren't locked down. I'd love your take on what feels good, and we can judge it from a small pilot before committing to the rest. The current faces are functional references, not a style you have to copy.
