Timing and ownership

Speech does not wait for the reader. A late proposal is discarded, as is a proposal overtaken by a new user turn or a face replacement. Explicit face requests take priority.

Look for affect_decision and speech_style in the saved trace. They show the proposal, evidence, elapsed time, rejection reason and setting actually sent to ElevenLabs.

Measurements

  • Speaker and reader comparison · authored conversation cases, real model calls, no microphone or playback measurement.
  • Independent review of the choices · wording, face selection and missed timing.
  • Listen to the voice samples · fixed sentence, existing MIST voice, provider timing and failures.
  • Live integration check · three typed turns through the real speaker, expression reader and voice stream.

These tests can show whether a proposal arrives in time and follows the contract. They do not establish that a conversation feels more human.

What ElevenLabs can change

The current Flash stream can vary stability per reply. The experimental profiles named warm, gentle, bright and serious are setting choices, not guaranteed emotions. All phrases in one reply use the same setting.

Eleven v3 supports expressive tags through a different dialogue streaming API. It produced audio with your MIST voice for plain text, [curious] and [excited]. Listen to the samples above. It is not the live studio's voice backend.
