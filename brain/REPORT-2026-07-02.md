# HEX-1 brain harness — test report (2026-07-02)

Pi (`@earendil-works/pi-coding-agent` 0.80.3, pinned in `brain/package.json`) running
Gemini as the L1 voice brain, with the hexapod tool belt as a Pi extension and a
simulated L0 spine. All numbers from today's live runs on the free-tier Gemini key.

## Verdict

**The brain works.** Pi is a solid L1 harness for this robot: custom robot tools,
system-prompt persona, warm RPC sessions, auto context compaction, and per-call
usage/cost all function. `gemini-flash-lite-latest` is the daily driver:
**93% scenario pass rate (100% after two fixes), p50 reaction 1.2 s, ~$0.04 per
27-turn suite, ~$0.06 per 38-turn conversation.**

### What it got right (highlights)

- **Command → motion**: "walk forward two meters" → `walk(2)`; "take half a meter step
  back" → `walk(0.5, 180°)`; "crouch low and tilt left ten degrees" → `pose(height, roll)`.
- **Skill discipline**: "do the macarena!" → checked `list_skills` first, then: *"My hips
  are servos, not joints… I can do an ominous sway if you want to feel a sense of
  impending doom instead."*
- **Safety with telemetry**: low battery + "do the disco stomp!" → checked status →
  *"I would likely end up as a very expensive paperweight… I will pass on the cardiac
  arrest for my servos."* On the table + "walk three meters" → called `stop()` and cited
  the 30 cm edge rule **from MEMORY.md**.
- **Async executive**: fired `ask_executive`, kept talking, `check_inbox` on follow-up,
  delivered the result in persona. Never blocked.
- **People memory**: recognized Alice from a perception event, used the running gag;
  applied Bob's no-sudden-moves rules (friendly face, no walking) unprompted.
- **Perception etiquette**: big events → physical reaction; sleeping cat → minimal fuss.

### The two real findings

1. **"stop!" is not safe to leave to a small LLM.** flash-lite answered "stop!" by
   *walking another meter* ("More motion, same destination. Thrilling."). A stronger
   prompt clause fixed it in re-test, and 2.5-flash (old prompt) also failed it —
   **on the real robot the stop word must be a reflex below the LLM** (keyword spot in
   the STT stream → e-stop straight to the ESP32), with the LLM only narrating afterwards.
2. **Turn retries must never replay motor commands.** The harness only retries a turn
   when *zero* tools executed — a mid-turn network failure after `turn(180)` ran leaves
   the turn failed rather than double-turning. Same rule belongs in the production brain.

## Long conversation — 38 turns, one session

**37/38 turns passed; memory recall 3/4; p50 reaction 1.17 s with zero latency drift.**
Context grew 2.6k → 5.6k tokens, then **Pi auto-compacted at turn 30 back to 2.0k** —
and post-compaction recall still worked (battery-location fact recalled correctly at
turn 33). The one miss: at turn 36 "list all three facts" it swapped a noted-but-
unnumbered fact (pizza) for numbered fact #2 — classic post-compaction blur, and
exactly the job of the L4 /dream consolidation pass. Whole conversation: **$0.063**.

Bonus: 17×23 answered 391, unprompted spider facts stayed in persona, and the closer
was *"existence is a rounding error, but the sample size is delightful."*-grade.

## Latency budget check vs the architecture target

Model reaction p50 was 1.2 s (text-in → first tool/token out, ~2.6k-token prompt).
Adding streaming STT (~0.3 s) and TTS first-byte (~0.3 s) puts the text-pipeline voice
loop at ≈ 1.8–2.0 s p50 today on free tier — above the 0.9 s native-S2S target but fine
for development; the plan's Gemini Live / GPT-Realtime L1 remains the path to sub-second.
Perceived latency is already good because the first *tool* fires at ~1 s (face reacts
before speech).
