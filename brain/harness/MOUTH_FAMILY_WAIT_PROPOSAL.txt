Measured concern

The browser energy fallback produces only AA/rest. On the six saved speech
clips, unchanged AA runs reach 2.16 s (long.wav), 1.28 s (precision), 1.26 s
(question), 1.18 s (excited), and 1.10 s (gentle). A sustained vowel can remain
AA indefinitely, so the present cue-boundary latch has no hard upper bound.
These are actual saved PCM measurements using the exact browser threshold,
not provider-alignment timing. See mouth_family_wait_analysis.json.

Proposed bounded handoff, not yet applied

Keep the existing immediate handoff on rest, MBP, or an actual viseme change.
For an unchanged cue, permit handoff at a quiet amplitude trough below 0.35
once 150 ms of the expression change has elapsed. If neither event arrives,
switch the same current viseme into its target emotional drawing at the first
15 fps tick at or after 400 ms from expression selection. Keep the same anchor,
display master width, rotation, current viseme and audio time. Only the emotional
contour changes, with one mouth draw and no forced silence or duplicate mouth.

This trades a bounded one-frame contour change during a very long vowel for
avoiding a happy mouth persisting under angry eyes. It remains possible to see
that contour change; no claim of smooth raster morphing is justified. The hard
bound would be 466.7 ms including 15 fps scheduling. Retargeting starts a fresh
pending target but never resets TTS or the displayed speech geometry.
