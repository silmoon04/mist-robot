Emotion mouth renderer contract

atlas.mouthFamilies maps each of the 40 face IDs to its assigned mouth asset.
If that map exists, the renderer never guesses a family from a face name.
The public helper mouthAssetForFace(faceId, atlas) returns the same assignment
used by production and the studio. Legacy atlases without a map retain the
previous neutral/smile mapping.

During live speech, an expression change queues the assigned family. A held
vowel keeps its current family until the next actual viseme boundary or a
rest/MBP closure, or a quiet amplitude below 0.35 after 150 ms. If the cue is
still unchanged, the first 15 fps tick at or after 400 ms changes the emotional
family while keeping that same viseme. Scheduling adds at most one 66.7 ms
frame. The latter change can be visible during a sustained vowel; it prevents
the previous emotion persisting indefinitely. No silent closure is inserted.
Geometry keeps moving while the family is pending. Only one mouth is drawn;
the TTS clock is not reset. Retargeting to a different family starts a new
bounded pending target. Stop or interruption clears that pending state.
Inspector pose overrides show the assigned family directly without changing
the live speech-family state. A new utterance and stopped speech reset the
pending family to the current expression. Canonical rests stay unchanged.

Nonhappy families resolve SMILE cues to their own EE or wide_close drawing,
so a generic speech cue cannot insert a happy mouth into an angry/sad face.
