Renderer timing contract for the 15 fps pass

Default visible animation cadence: 15 fps (66.6667 ms per animation frame).
Expression placement, eye pose and gaze sample a shared animation frame clock.
Default expression transition: 600 ms (9 frames). Requested durations round up
to whole animation frames, with a 400 ms minimum and 1600 ms maximum.
Blink duration: 400 ms. Closed interval: 100 ms to 233.3333 ms, a 133.3333 ms
hold. A normal blink is not forced to display every available cel.
The speech sampler always reads the current audible audio time. It is never
advanced by frame count. Draws occur at 15 fps; inactive speech, rest, MBP and
stopSpeech can close the mouth immediately between scheduled draws.
renderTo keeps its current API. Held inspector overrides force a draw. A fps
option can set a deliberate preview cadence; the default remains 15.
The base renderer includes a TH-to-LNT compatibility alias. The drawn renderer
loads replacement frames from their declared source file and applies their
sourceScale once. The production and inspector copies use identical code.
