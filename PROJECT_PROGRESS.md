The purchasing docs contain older wheel-leg plans and dated prices; the latest assembly checklist is the mechanical reference.

Publication lesson: keep measured physical results distinct from CAD checks, slicer estimates and prepared files. This follows the saved inspection handoff and prevents later releases from upgrading an unknown print result into a success claim.
